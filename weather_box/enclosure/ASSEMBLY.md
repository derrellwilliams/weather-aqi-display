# Assembly — three printed parts, four screws, two straps

## Buy / gather

- Four **M3 × 30 mm machine screws**, 0.5 mm pitch, Phillips pan head or hex socket head. Head diameter must be no more than 6 mm, head height no more than 3 mm. Length is measured under the head. No countersunk screws, wood screws or self-tapping screws.
- Four **standard M3 hex nuts**, 5.5 mm across flats, 2.4 mm thick. Not locknuts, flange nuts or heat-set inserts.
- Thin, non-adhesive, reusable **back-to-back hook-and-loop tape** (hooks on one side, loops on the other). Cut two strips approximately **6 mm wide × 150 mm long**, then trim excess after fitting. Choose tape no more than approximately 1.2 mm thick. Plain continuous tape is easier to thread than pre-cut ties with bulky heads. Overlap at least 25 mm. Slots are 8 × 2.6 mm; trim wider tape lengthwise to 6 mm.
- A few small pieces of **0.4 mm thick electronics-safe double-sided cushioning tape** for the screen perimeter. Keep tape off the active image, ribbon and components. Thick household foam mounting tape changes the fit.
- Your four adhesive ferrite magnets, nominal Ø18.03 × 3.05 mm.
- Small screwdriver or Allen key to suit the screw heads.

Amazon search terms: “M3 x 30mm pan head machine screws nuts”, “M3 hex nuts 5.5mm 2.4mm”, and “thin back to back hook loop tape roll”. Confirm dimensions above in the actual product listing. No particular Amazon seller or listing is required.

## Before inserting electronics

1. Remove print burrs and stringing. Check screw holes and strap slots are open. Do all scraping/filing away from electronics.
2. If you print the optional coupon, test that an M3 nut drops into the raised shell-corner pocket, the screw slides through the round hole, its head fits the round counterbore, a magnet drops into the shallow circular pocket, and the trimmed strap threads through the slot. Do not force a tight part.
3. Dry-assemble the EMPTY shell, carrier and back. The carrier's four hollow corner columns meet the inside back. The four M3 nuts drop into the shell's interior corner pockets. Screws enter from the back, pass through the carrier columns and engage the nuts. Tighten only gently by hand. The cover should sit flat without bending. If a screw bottoms out before closing or hardware does not fit, stop and adjust the model/hardware rather than tightening harder.
4. Remove the cover and carrier. Keep the front shell face-down on a clean, soft surface. All electronics must be unpowered: unplug USB and battery.

## Screen and carrier

5. Compare the screen with `screen-fit-template.pdf`, printed at actual size. The 100 mm reference bar must measure 100 mm. Check the window does not cover active pixels before using adhesive.
6. Place small cushioning-tape tabs on the front ledge behind the non-image perimeter. Use at least four short tabs, avoiding the left-side ribbon exit. Screen front faces outward; the wide blank margin goes to the wide internal left pocket. The screen should drop into the alignment fence with clearance, never be forced or flexed.
7. Thread the two straps through the carrier BEFORE fitting it. Each goes down one slot, along the underside, and up the other slot, forming a loop anchored to the carrier. The long slot pair beside the board rails is for the board/XIAO; the pair across the battery pocket is for the battery.
8. Seat the driver board on its two narrow edge rails, components facing the back. The screen connector faces the open ribbon channel; USB faces inward. Confirm no support presses a solder joint or pin. The two new corner stops at the ribbon end oppose USB insertion force. Lower the board between both sets of end stops rather than sliding it in. Confirm the central ribbon connector/latch clears the corner stops; its exact outline was not measured. Keep the XIAO fully seated. Wrap the 6 mm strap across its metal shield, avoiding the buttons, antenna connector and USB socket. Fasten the strap onto itself, just enough to prevent lifting; do not bend the PCB.
9. Put the battery in the shallow pocket. Its 49.8 mm direction runs across the case, and the taped/wire end faces toward the driver board. Pass its strap over the smooth pouch, away from its wire exit. Fasten only snug enough to prevent movement; do not indent or compress the pouch. Keep the overlap away from the USB access path. Battery and board remain independently strapped when the cover is removed.
10. Lower the carrier into the shell gently, keeping the screen ribbon in the carrier's open side channel. Connect it only when the board is close enough that the ribbon remains relaxed. Never pull on the ribbon, crease it or force it against the shell. The screen is not a structural support for the carrier.
11. Route the battery lead through the open aisle beside its pocket. Check actual reach without tension; then verify polarity at the board's + mark before connecting. Keep all wires above the carrier and out of the cover seam and screw columns.
12. Attach the antenna with its existing adhesive to the inside top wall, in the space shown in `review.html`. The carrier has a notch below it. Keep its cable clear of the strap and controls.

## Back, magnets and charging

13. Check that your USB cable can be fully inserted with the cover removed, passing over the battery without pushing it or the board. The modeled plug clearance is a 35 × 14 × 8 mm volume, not a measurement of your cable. Check reset/boot and the power switch remain accessible with the back off.
14. Put the back on and insert the four screws. Tighten gently in a diagonal sequence until the cover seats. The screws load the carrier's corner columns, not the screen or battery. If anything rattles or the cover resists closing, open it and correct the fit; do not clamp it shut.
15. Stick each magnet into an outside recess. All four should protrude beyond the screw heads. Use a thin non-scratch covering on contact faces if needed, and test grip on your fridge over a soft surface. Do not assume the magnet count guarantees sufficient holding force.
16. To charge: take the case off the fridge, lay it screen-down on a clean soft surface, remove the four screws and back, and plug in USB. Keep the carrier seated. If removing the carrier for service, first disconnect/relieve attached cables so you do not pull the ribbon or antenna lead. Do not lift the carrier out with the short screen ribbon attached.
